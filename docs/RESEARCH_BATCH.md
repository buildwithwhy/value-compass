# Research batch — ten operators, four question groups

Started 2026-09-27. Same questions, same process, for every company in the batch, so
differences between them reflect the companies rather than how hard we looked.

**Batch:** OpenAI · Anthropic · Alphabet/Google · Microsoft · Meta · Proton ·
Lovable · Replit · StackBlitz · Vercel

**Question groups**

- **A — Who benefits financially?** Operator and parent, disclosed ownership interests,
  announced investors, named commercial suppliers.
- **B — Who makes the decisions?** Voting control, board appointment and removal, founder
  rights, the actual authority of any foundation or trust.
- **C — What does it contribute beyond its own products?** Maintained open-source projects,
  grants, donations, public-benefit commitments and evidence of fulfilment.
- **D — How has it treated people affected by its work?** Workers including contractors and
  data workers, creators and the use of their work, communities affected by operations.
  Window: roughly the last three years, with older ongoing matters included and labelled.

**Rules for this batch**

- A pledge, a binding obligation and a completed contribution are three different things.
- An announced investment is not an ownership percentage. Voting power is not an economic
  stake. A named supplier does not tell us how much of a payment reaches it.
- Allegation, investigation, judgment, settlement and established fact are distinguished, and
  current status is checked where possible.
- No controversy counting. Finding nothing is not a clean record, and more recorded matters
  is not automatically worse conduct — coverage varies with company size and scrutiny.

## What was already held versus newly researched

**Already held, but not surfaced.** Backers for all ten; product-level ownership and control
findings for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Meta AI and Lumo; supplier lists for Replit and
Duck.ai; open-source contributions for StackBlitz, Vercel and Replit. These were recorded
against products or as `lead_backers` and never reached the company profiles, which is why
profiles said nothing was established while the same page listed investors.

**Newly researched in this batch.** Group D across all ten, and group C for the companies
where it was missing. Group A for Lovable, Alibaba and DuckDuckGo.

**Operators found to have no directory record at all.** Proton, Alibaba and DuckDuckGo each
operate a product in the comparison but had no company page, so their evidence sat on the
product record with nothing to carry it. All three now have records. A test asserts that every
operator in the comparison has one, so this cannot recur silently.

**Dropped in verification.** A search summary asserted an April 2026 Lovable source-code
exposure and a "VibeScamming" finding. Neither appears in the cited source, so neither is
published.

## Retrieval blockers

Recorded so the same blocked route is not retried:

- `openai.com` and `help.openai.com` return HTTP 403 to automated fetches. Official pages read
  through search indexing of them instead.
- `perplexity.ai` help-centre paths return 403.
- `learn.replit.com` is JavaScript-rendered and returns an empty shell.
- `meta.com` and `transparency.meta.com` render client-side; Help Centre articles fetch fine.

A retrieval failure is recorded as such and is never treated as evidence that a capability or
fact does not exist.
